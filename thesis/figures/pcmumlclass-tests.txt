The annotated sources alone do not compile on purpose: the reactions compiler guards
against '@feature' in its input ("Found '@feature' annotation in reactions source. Run the
reactions preprocessor before compiling, ...") and reported that error for all 30 annotated
rule files, the same guard the annotated umljava master relies on. Every build of annotated
rules therefore goes through the preprocessor, and the preprocessed column measures the
transferred pipeline end to end.
